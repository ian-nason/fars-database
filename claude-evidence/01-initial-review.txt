The "each crash reconciles" claim in `CODEX-REVIEW.md:7` and `README.md:20` is therefore stronger than the check enforces. ## What I confirmed from the supplied text

- **Types and missingness.** Column typing, the all-VARCHAR CSV read with blank-to-NULL only for numeric columns, and the per-column pinned blank counts are internally consistent. Blank counts in `schema.json` match `build-report.json` null counts and the 10.2 percent dictionary figures. The `typed` parser rejects dots, NaN, fractional integers and over-precision, with fixtures for each.
- **Keys and relationships.** Keys match the manual's unique identifiers. Person type 3 is excluded from the in-transport vehicle join and checked for non-collision with transport vehicle numbers. Nonoccupants and parked-vehicle occupants are retained by LEFT JOINs. The crash view partitions people correctly given the vehicle-number semantics check at `fars.py:92`.
- **Field handling.** Age, coordinate, injury and BAC code domains match the manual pages supplied, including the 940 top-code flag, 995-999 nonmeasurement codes, and the 2018-later coordinate codes. The exact-decimal BAC scaling is fixture-tested.
- **Counts.** Column totals (80+201+126+6=413), row totals (190,798), record totals (180,634), 30 label fields, six example queries and 39 fixtures (17+19+3) all reconcile across README, DICTIONARY, build report, test output and the HF card. Peak RSS and elapsed time in the Codex review match `evidence/release-build/resources.json`.
- **Provenance and license.** Release notes confirm the first 2024 release on 2026-04-01. The license basis is NHTSA's own ownership statement, honestly recorded with the 403 on direct fetch. Archive and member hashes are consistent between `source-lock.json` and `sources-2024.json`, and build input hashes match between the build report, the test input manifest and the snapshot.
- **Resource bounds and fresh checkout.** Output and download paths are confined to the repository, partials are never accepted, and the supervisor limits match the README. The fresh-checkout directory fix has a fixture.
